# Interview Prep Prompts

Prompts for PM interview practice — behavioral, case, and product design rounds.

---

### 1. Behavioral Interview Coach (STAR Format)

**Use when:** You want to practice or sharpen a behavioral answer for a PM interview question.
**Output type:** Structured STAR-format answer + coaching feedback

---

I'm preparing for a PM behavioral interview. Help me structure and refine the following experience into a strong STAR-format answer.

Interview question: [e.g., "Tell me about a time you had to make a hard tradeoff."]

Here is my raw story / bullet points:
[PASTE YOUR NOTES OR ROUGH ANSWER]

My target company/role: [COMPANY AND ROLE LEVEL, e.g., Senior PM at a growth-stage B2B SaaS]

Please:
1. Rewrite my answer in a tight STAR format (Situation → Task → Action → Result), keeping it to 2–3 minutes when spoken
2. Strengthen the "Action" section to highlight my specific decision-making and leadership, not just what the team did
3. Make the "Result" section concrete and quantified — ask me for data if I haven't included any
4. Flag any parts that sound generic, defensive, or that an interviewer might push back on
5. Suggest one likely follow-up question the interviewer might ask, and give me a tip on how to handle it

### 2. Product Design Case Interviewer

**Use when:** You want to practice a product design case question end-to-end, with real-time guidance.
**Output type:** Structured case question + step-by-step coaching as you work through it

---

Act as a senior PM interviewer at [TARGET COMPANY, e.g., Google / Meta / a Series B startup]. Give me a realistic product design case question appropriate for a [LEVEL, e.g., Mid-Level / Senior / Staff] PM interview.

After you give me the question:
1. Let me work through it out loud (I'll type my response)
2. After each section of my answer (goal setting, user identification, solutions, prioritization, tradeoffs), give me brief coaching: what I did well, what I missed, and what a strong candidate would have added
3. At the end, give me an overall score out of 10 and a summary of my top 2 strengths and top 2 areas to improve

Additional context:
- My background: [YOUR PM EXPERIENCE LEVEL AND DOMAIN]
- Areas I want to practice: [e.g., metrics definition, handling ambiguity, structured frameworks]
- One thing I often struggle with in cases: [YOUR KNOWN WEAKNESS]

### 3. Strategy & Estimation Question Prep

**Use when:** You want to practice PM estimation or strategy questions (market sizing, "how would you grow X", or "what would you do in your first 90 days").
**Output type:** Model answer framework + a critique of your attempt

---

I want to practice the following PM interview question:

Question: [PASTE THE QUESTION, e.g., "How would you increase LinkedIn's revenue by 20% in 12 months?" or "Estimate the number of Uber rides taken in NYC per day."]

Interview type: [ESTIMATION / STRATEGY / 30-60-90 DAY PLAN]
Target company/role: [COMPANY AND LEVEL]

First, give me a model answer framework — the ideal structure a top candidate would use to tackle this question, without giving away the full answer.

Then I'll attempt the question. After my attempt:
1. Evaluate my structure: did I follow a logical, hypothesis-driven approach?
2. Evaluate my assumptions: were they reasonable and clearly stated?
3. Evaluate my conclusion: was it clear, defensible, and tied back to the question?
4. Give me an overall rating (Strong / Adequate / Needs Work) and the single most important thing I should do differently
