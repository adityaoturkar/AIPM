# Discovery Prompts

Prompts for user research, problem framing, and opportunity identification.

---

### 1. Problem Statement Framer

**Use when:** You have raw user feedback or observations and need to synthesize them into a clear problem statement.
**Output type:** Structured problem statement + supporting evidence summary

---

I have gathered the following user research and feedback for [PRODUCT/FEATURE AREA]:

[PASTE RAW NOTES, INTERVIEW QUOTES, SURVEY DATA, OR SUPPORT TICKETS HERE]

Help me synthesize this into a sharp problem statement using the format:
"[USER PERSONA] struggle to [DO THING] because [ROOT CAUSE], which leads to [NEGATIVE OUTCOME]."

Then provide:
1. The top 3 supporting evidence points from the data I shared
2. Any gaps or contradictions in the research I should investigate further
3. A list of assumptions embedded in the problem statement that need validation

---

### 2. Discovery Interview Question Generator

**Use when:** You are preparing for user discovery interviews and need a structured question guide.
**Output type:** Interview guide with warm-up, core, and closing questions

---

I'm conducting discovery interviews for [PRODUCT/FEATURE AREA]. My hypothesis is that [TARGET USERS] experience [ASSUMED PROBLEM] when trying to [JOB TO BE DONE].

Generate a 45-minute interview guide with:
- 3 warm-up questions to build rapport and understand the user's context
- 5–7 open-ended core questions that explore the problem space without leading the witness (avoid yes/no questions and avoid mentioning our solution)
- 2 questions that challenge my hypothesis — designed to surface disconfirming evidence
- 2 closing questions that uncover priorities and willingness to change
- A note on what to probe deeper on if the user mentions [SPECIFIC SIGNAL OR TOPIC]

Target user: [USER ROLE/PERSONA]
Company context: [B2B/B2C, INDUSTRY, COMPANY SIZE IF RELEVANT]

---

### 3. Opportunity Sizing Quick Brief

**Use when:** You need a rough opportunity sizing framework before committing to deeper research.
**Output type:** Structured opportunity brief with TAM/SAM/SOM framing and data prompts

---

I am evaluating whether to invest in solving the following problem: [PROBLEM DESCRIPTION].

Potential target users: [USER SEGMENT]
Our current product/context: [BRIEF PRODUCT DESCRIPTION]

Help me create an opportunity sizing brief that covers:
1. A top-down market estimate using TAM → SAM → SOM logic, with the key assumptions I need to validate and suggested data sources for each
2. A bottom-up estimate based on our existing user base: if [X% OF OUR USERS] have this problem and we could capture [Y% OF THAT], what does that look like in terms of [METRIC: revenue / DAU / retention]?
3. The 3 biggest unknowns that would most change this estimate
4. A recommended next step to validate the most critical assumption within [TIMEFRAME]
